I am a first-year Ph.D. student at the Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering, University of Washington. I received my undergraduate degree from the Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (IIIS, Yao Class) at Tsinghua University.

My research focuses on recursively self-improving agent systems. I am particularly interested in:

  • Self-evolving agent systems that create, curate, and reuse their own tools and skills (Alita, Alita-G, Survey of Self-Evolving Agents)
  • Agentic reinforcement learning and post-training for long-horizon agents
  • Agent memory and agent world models (PORTAL, Understanding Is Done Early)

Internships & Research:

  • Xiaohongshu HI Lab, Pretraining Department (Mar–Aug 2026): recursively self-improving AI agent harness and post-training (ACE Top Intern Program)
  • Reinforce Omega AI Start-up (Sept 2025–Feb 2026): AI agent workflows, tool-use systems, and agent memory
  • University of Pennsylvania, Cognitive Computation Group (May–Aug 2025): Demystifying Supervision Data Generalization in Multimodal LMs (Advisor: Prof. Dan Roth)
  • Princeton University, Wang Lab (Nov 2024–Feb 2026): Co-led Alita and Alita-G (Advisor: Prof. Mengdi Wang)

I serve as a reviewer for ICLR 2026/2027, NeurIPS 2026, ICML 2026, COLM 2026, AAAI 2026, and ACL Rolling Review. Outside research, I am a passionate Go player (3rd place, San Yi Cup National Dan Promotion Tournament).
